Hi, I'm Soumya - a college student from India in my early 20's, currently pursuing a bachelor's degree. Although right now I guess I am the blinking cursor on the screen, forming words to convey my thoughts to you, the reader. You may or may not already know me from my previous online presence around a few minecraft bedrock communities. Now that we're (im)properly introduced, let's get to the point.

This is my blog where I will post stuff I think I should share with the world, or rather however minuscule percentage of it will read through these. Blogging is in new territory for me but it doesn't hurt to try out new things. I will attempt to keep my blogs short and concise with an informal conversational tone for attention spans in current gen's standard (oh wait that amount's still extremely optimistic). Even then expect one post per whenever I feel like it, as I don't want to turn it into another burden eventually being lead to abandonment - just like dad who went to go grab some milk. Pardon my humour if it's too annoying. Also, thanks to faizul for letting me know about chirpy blog, setting it up was more straightforward than expected.

Let's wrap up for today, as it seems sufficient enough as the first post. Instead of trying to make it as best as I can, I tried to acknowledge the imperfections in my writing as I believe that one's one is defined by both one's virtues and flaws. If you couldn't already tell - no form of AI was used in the making of this post, and I don't intend to ever do that in future.
